How the Recommendation Engine Works

This system is a Machine Learning & NLP-based recommendation system utilizing Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) and Cosine Similarity.

1. TF-IDF Vectorization

Converts job descriptions, skills, and candidate profile texts into high-dimensional numerical vectors, giving higher weights to discriminative technical terms.

2. Cosine Similarity

Measures the cosine of the angle between the candidate vector and each job vector in the vector space, assessing semantic match independent of document length.

Multi-Factor Scoring Formula
Final Match Score =
  70% × Content Cosine Similarity
+ 15% × Preferred Job Role Match
+ 10% × Preferred Location Match
+ 05% × Experience Compatibility
